Our Programs & Memberships

At Kings Martial Arts, we offer programs for students of all ages and experience levels. Our goal is to help every student develop confidence, discipline, strength, and respect.

Our Martial Arts Programs

Little Dragons (Ages 4-6)

Our youngest students learn the basics of martial arts through fun activities that develop coordination, listening skills, confidence, and discipline.

Junior Martial Arts (Ages 7-12)

Students develop martial arts fundamentals, teamwork, respect, fitness, and basic self-defense techniques.

Teen Martial Arts (Ages 13-17)

Our teen program focuses on improving strength, confidence, discipline, and advanced martial arts techniques.

Adult Martial Arts (Ages 18+)

Adults can improve their fitness, learn practical self-defense, build strength, and develop new skills in a welcoming training environment.
