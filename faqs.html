Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about getting started with martial arts? We are here to help! Below are answers to some of the most common questions about joining Kings Martial Arts.

1. Do I need previous martial arts experience?

No! Beginners are welcome. Our programs are designed for students of all experience levels, and our instructors will help you learn at your own pace.

2. What age can someone start martial arts?

We offer classes for children as young as 4 years old, as well as programs for teens and adults. There are opportunities for students of different ages and skill levels.

3. What should I wear to my first class?

Comfortable athletic clothing is perfect for your first class. As you continue training, our instructors can explain what uniform or equipment you may need.

4. How much do memberships cost?

We offer several membership options to fit different schedules and training goals. Visit our Programs & Memberships page to learn more about our plans and pricing.

5. How often should I attend classes?

We recommend attending classes consistently to build skills and confidence. Students can choose a membership that fits their schedule and training goals.

6. Is martial arts safe for children?

Safety is an important part of our training. Classes are structured around age-appropriate instruction, respect, control, and proper technique.

7. Will martial arts help improve fitness?

Yes! Martial arts can help improve strength, flexibility, balance, coordination, endurance, and overall fitness.

8. Can parents watch their children's classes?

Parents and guardians are encouraged to stay involved in their child's martial arts journey. Ask our staff about viewing arrangements at the studio.

9. What should I bring to class?

Bring a water bottle, comfortable clothing for your first class, and a positive attitude. Your instructor will let you know if additional equipment is needed.

10. How do I sign up?

Getting started is easy! Visit our Programs & Memberships page to explore your options, or check our Schedule page to see the available class times.

Still Have Questions?

We would love to help you get started on your martial arts journey. Explore our programs and find a class that works for you!
